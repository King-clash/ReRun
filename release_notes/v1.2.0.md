# Rerun 1.2.0

Rerun is a helper program that plays Grand Summoners for you on BlueStacks. Version 1.2.0 is the biggest change so far: Rerun is no longer only an auto-farmer. It now has an **Account Manager** (a place that keeps track of your account) and a separate **Macro Farming** area (where all the farming controls live). Download **Rerun-1.2.0.zip** below, unzip it, and run `Rerun.exe`. Your existing data is not touched: your current unit list becomes the first account, called "Main".

**Please read first:** Grand Summoners may not allow macros, bots or automation. Using Rerun can lead to account limits, suspension or a permanent ban. Rerun now shows this warning at the top of the Macro Farming area all the time. The safety limits it offers only cap how much Rerun runs; they do not make it safe.

## What changed

**1. Account Manager (the first tab).**
- You can keep several accounts. Each has its own numbers, to-do list, goals, crests, units, limits and Discord settings. Nothing is shared between accounts.
- Every number remembers where it came from: typed by you, read from the game, or unverified. A game reading that Rerun is not sure about is set aside instead of replacing a number you trust. You can also lock a number so only you change it.
- **Scanning** reads your energy, gems, gold and Rank (your player level) from the bar at the top of the game. Alch, tickets, your player name and your inventory cannot be read yet, so you type those in.
- A **daily and weekly checklist** resets at the game's reset time (6 AM, UTC-6), not at your computer's midnight. You can tick tasks off yourself. "Login reward" can be started by Rerun (the Login & Mail script); a task Rerun finishes stays marked "Not yet verified" until you confirm it.
- **Goals** such as "save 30,000 gems" or "collect 3 crests" show progress and a suggestion of what to farm, with the reason. Rerun never spends gems, summons or anything else by itself.
- A **Crest Vault** lists your crests, lets you add the sub-stats yourself, and gives advice (keep, review, possible duplicate, consider discarding) from rules you set. It only advises. Rerun cannot scrap or sell anything from here.
- **Events** you add by hand with reminders to Discord, a **daily report** per account, and per-account limits and alert settings.

**2. Macro Farming (the second tab).**
- Your dashboard, routine settings and history moved here, plus a list of every routine (marked Verified or Beta), **Dry Run**, session history per account, daily and per-run time limits, and an **Emergency Stop** button (F12 still works).
- **Dry Run** lets a routine run but sends no taps. Rerun lists what it would have tapped instead.
- If two accounts share one BlueStacks window, Rerun asks which account is open before it starts, because it cannot see that by itself.

**3. Smaller changes.**
- The old Settings tab now only holds app-wide settings. The per-routine settings are under Macro Farming > Routine settings.
- The window is a little taller at minimum (800 pixels) to fit the warning and account bar.
- The program file is smaller (an unused image plugin was left out), and the folder of debug screenshots keeps only the newest 60.

## What has and has not been checked

- **Checked automatically:** all 31 groups of tests pass, including new ones for accounts, scanning and both new screens. The top-bar reader was tried on 26 saved screenshots from the real game and was never wrong, but they are all from one account, and gold was only seen at the 999,999,999 cap.
- **Not checked on the real game:** nearly all of the new features, including running a scan against a live BlueStacks window, Dry Run on a real emulator, and the checklist starting the Login & Mail script. Treat them as BETA.
- **Not built:** automatic switching between accounts (Rerun cannot tell which account is logged in), readers for Alch, tickets, player name and inventory, and Discord slash commands that know about accounts.
- The weekly reset day is assumed to be Monday; you can change it per account.
- The file is still not code-signed, so Microsoft Defender may still complain.

## How to install

1. Close Rerun if it is running. Copy your old folder somewhere safe as a backup first.
2. Unzip **Rerun-1.2.0.zip** and run `Rerun.exe`. Putting it in your existing Rerun folder (replacing the old `Rerun.exe`) keeps your data.
3. New accounts are stored in an `accounts` folder next to Rerun.
